FARMER GUIDE

Protect Your Crops

Early identification of pests and diseases can help farmers protect crops and reduce losses.

🌾
Paddy

Stem Borer

Stem borers can damage rice stems and may cause dead hearts or white ears.

🔎 Symptoms Dead heart, drying central shoots and white ears.
🛡️ Prevention Monitor the crop regularly and maintain proper field management.
☁️
Cotton

Bollworm

Bollworms can attack cotton squares, flowers and developing bolls.

🔎 Symptoms Holes in squares and bolls, damaged flowers and larvae.
🛡️ Prevention Regular crop scouting and integrated pest management.
🌶️
Chilli

Thrips

Thrips can damage young leaves and growing parts of chilli plants.

🔎 Symptoms Leaf curling, silvering and distorted young growth.
🛡️ Prevention Monitor young leaves and maintain good field sanitation.
🍅
Tomato

Fruit Borer

Fruit borers can damage tomato fruits and reduce marketable yield.

🔎 Symptoms Circular holes in fruits and feeding damage.
🛡️ Prevention Remove damaged fruits and monitor the crop frequently.
🥜
Groundnut

Leaf Miner

Leaf miners can create tunnels inside leaves and reduce healthy leaf area.

🔎 Symptoms Winding trails and mines visible on leaves.
🛡️ Prevention Monitor leaves regularly and remove severely damaged plant parts.
🟠
Turmeric

Rhizome Rot

Excess moisture and poor drainage can contribute to rhizome rot problems.

🔎 Symptoms Yellowing leaves, wilting and rotting rhizomes.
🛡️ Prevention Ensure good drainage and use healthy planting material.
🌽
Maize

Fall Armyworm

Fall armyworm mainly damages maize leaves and can affect young plants.

🔎 Symptoms Ragged leaf damage and feeding injury around the whorl.
🛡️ Prevention Regularly inspect the crop, especially the whorl.
🌿
Red Gram

Pod Borer

Pod borers can damage flowers and pods, reducing pulse yield.

🔎 Symptoms Holes in pods and damaged seeds.
🛡️ Prevention Regular scouting and integrated pest management practices.
❌ No pest or crop found.
⚠️

Important Farmer Note

Pest and disease symptoms can look similar. Correct identification is important before choosing any control measure. For serious crop damage, consult a qualified agricultural expert or local agriculture department.
