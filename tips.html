🌱 Farming Tips

Simple farming practices for healthier crops

SMART FARMING

Better Practices, Better Farming

Good planning, proper crop management and timely field activities can help farmers improve crop health and productivity.

01
🌱

Soil Preparation

Prepare the soil properly before sowing. Good soil structure helps roots develop and improves water movement.

  • ✔ Remove unwanted weeds
  • ✔ Maintain suitable soil moisture
  • ✔ Use soil testing when possible
02
🌾

Quality Seeds

Use good-quality seed suitable for the local climate, soil and farming season.

  • ✔ Choose suitable varieties
  • ✔ Check seed quality
  • ✔ Follow recommended seed rate
03
💧

Smart Irrigation

Provide water according to crop growth stage, soil moisture and weather conditions.

  • ✔ Avoid unnecessary irrigation
  • ✔ Check soil moisture
  • ✔ Maintain proper drainage
04
🧪

Fertilizer Management

Apply nutrients according to soil condition and crop requirements instead of overusing fertilizers.

  • ✔ Prefer soil-test-based decisions
  • ✔ Apply nutrients at suitable stages
  • ✔ Avoid excessive fertilizer use
05
🌿

Weed Management

Weeds compete with crops for water, nutrients, sunlight and growing space.

  • ✔ Inspect fields regularly
  • ✔ Control weeds at the right stage
  • ✔ Keep field boundaries clean
06
🐛

Pest Monitoring

Regular crop inspection helps identify pest problems early.

  • ✔ Check leaves and stems
  • ✔ Monitor damaged plant parts
  • ✔ Use integrated pest management
07
🌦️

Weather Planning

Weather conditions can affect sowing, irrigation, spraying and harvesting.

  • ✔ Monitor rainfall forecasts
  • ✔ Plan field work carefully
  • ✔ Protect crops during extreme weather
08
🚜

Harvesting

Harvest crops at the appropriate maturity stage to maintain quality and reduce losses.

  • ✔ Check crop maturity
  • ✔ Avoid harvesting during wet conditions
  • ✔ Handle produce carefully
09
🏠

Crop Storage

Proper storage can help protect harvested produce from moisture, pests and damage.

  • ✔ Keep storage areas clean
  • ✔ Control moisture
  • ✔ Inspect stored produce regularly
10
🍃

Organic Practices

Organic matter and sustainable farming practices can help maintain soil health.

  • ✔ Add suitable organic matter
  • ✔ Maintain soil cover where appropriate
  • ✔ Encourage beneficial organisms
11
📒

Keep Farm Records

Maintaining records can help farmers understand costs, inputs and crop performance.

  • ✔ Record input costs
  • ✔ Record crop activities
  • ✔ Track yield and selling price
12
🧑‍🌾

Farmer Safety

Use appropriate protective equipment and follow product labels when handling agricultural inputs.

  • ✔ Read product labels
  • ✔ Use protective equipment
  • ✔ Store inputs safely
